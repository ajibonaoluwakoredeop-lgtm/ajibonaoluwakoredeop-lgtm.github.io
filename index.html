01 · Biography

About

I am a researcher in the Department of Philosophy at the University of Kansas, with research interests in African political philosophy, the ethics and governance of artificial intelligence, and the social and political implications of digital technologies.

My work examines how technological systems shape power, democratic life, development, and relations between the Global North and Global South. I am particularly interested in African and other Global South perspectives on responsible AI development, digital colonialism, and technology governance.

I am also affiliated with the University of Kansas Center for Cyber-Social Dynamics (CCSD).

02 · Areas of inquiry

Research interests

African Political Philosophy

Political thought, social transformation, democracy, and African intellectual traditions.

AI Ethics and Governance

Responsible AI development, accountability, regulation, and the distribution of technological benefits and risks.

Digital Colonialism

Platform power, digital sovereignty, data extraction, and technology relations between the Global North and South.

Technology and Environmental Justice

The environmental and socioeconomic consequences of AI infrastructure and digital development.

03 · Current themes

Research projects

AI governance · Africa

Forecasting Socio-Economic Futures of AI in Africa

Research agenda on the social and economic futures of AI in Africa, with attention to inclusive governance, local knowledge, and institutional capacity.

AI governance · Brazil

Towards a Globally Responsible Development of AI: Exploring Brazil as a Key Player

Examines Brazil's role in global AI governance and debates about digital colonialism, platform power, and regulatory autonomy.

Democracy · Environment

AI, Democracy and Environmental Justice in Africa

Examines the democratic and environmental implications of AI infrastructure and pathways toward more participatory technology governance. Related article published in The Paris Journal on AI & Digital Ethics (2026).

Digital humanities · Lagos

Lagos Data Center Justice Project

Developing an interdisciplinary project on digital infrastructure, environmental justice, housing, and social inequality in Lagos, Nigeria, using digital mapping, databases, and community knowledge.
